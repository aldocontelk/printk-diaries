About

Hi, I’m Aldo Conte and i’m from Italy! I live in Turin.

I’m an electronics engineer, and what has always fascinated me is that thin layer between a physical object and the software that controls it: how a piece of digital hardware is designed so that software can shape its behavior down to the smallest detail.

I have another big passion, which is mountains and all the outdoor activities they offer, as well as gravel biking.

Printk Diaries is the logbook where i write down what i learn along the way: kernel patches and the lessons behind them, driver development, debugging techniques, testing tricks with and without hardware, and everything that sits between electronics and the kernel. Here, though, expect to read about just about anything that I’m passionate about and that’s worth sharing.
